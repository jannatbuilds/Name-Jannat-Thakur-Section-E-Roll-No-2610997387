Famous Indian Foods

Indian cuisine is known for its rich flavours, colourful dishes, and variety of spices. Every region of India has its own unique food traditions and special dishes. Popular foods include biryani, dosa, idli, paneer tikka, rajma chawal, and samosas.It reflects the diverse culture and traditions of India and is loved by people all around the world.
